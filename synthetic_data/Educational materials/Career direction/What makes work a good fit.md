A job title can hide very different day-to-day work. Describe a good fit in terms you could observe during a normal week.

## Turn preferences into questions

| Preference | Question to investigate |
| --- | --- |
| Time to focus | How much of a typical week is spent in meetings or responding to urgent requests? |
| Room to learn | What has someone on this team learned in their first six months? |
| Clear ownership | Who decides which work takes priority when plans change? |

## Example

Mara, a fictional scheduling coordinator, is curious about project operations. She enjoys untangling handoffs and keeping work moving. She has less energy for work that depends on being available all evening. She wants to learn how often the roles she is considering require out-of-hours work.

Ask the client to mark each preference as a **requirement**, a **preference**, or **something to test**. Pick one uncertain preference and write down what evidence would clarify it.
