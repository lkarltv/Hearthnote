Transferable skills are easier to recognize when they describe an action in context. Start with a moment the client remembers, then ask what they did and what happened.

## From a duty to an example

- **Duty:** Helped onboard volunteers.
- **Action:** Rewrote the first-shift guide and paired each new volunteer with an experienced teammate.
- **Observed result:** New volunteers arrived knowing who to ask and which tasks they could start independently.
- **Possible skills:** Clear documentation, onboarding design, coordination.

## Make the wording specific

Ask which parts the client personally handled and what evidence supports the result. If they have no reliable numbers, describe the outcome in plain language. Then compare the example with a task in a role they are considering. The client may recognize a skill they can already demonstrate or a gap worth exploring.
