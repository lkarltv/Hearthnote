Use **Situation, Task, Action, Result** to shape an answer to a question like “Tell me about a time you improved a process.”

| Part | Fictional example |
| --- | --- |
| Situation | A small community program tracked room requests across two inboxes. |
| Task | Lee was responsible for finding a way to prevent double bookings. |
| Action | Lee mapped where requests got lost, tried a shared register, and showed colleagues how to log and confirm bookings. |
| Result | The team could see booking status in one place during the trial and chose to keep using it. |

When rehearsing, give enough context for the listener to follow. Spend most of the answer on the decision you made and the steps you took. Ask the listener what they remember about your contribution.
