Use a short conversation to learn how a role works in practice. Pick one thing the client is genuinely curious about before they reach out.

## Sample outreach

> Hi Rowan, I'm exploring a move from events coordination into operations. I'd like to learn how your team handles handoffs between departments. Would you have 20 minutes for a short call next week? Thanks for considering it.

## Questions to bring

1. Which tasks take up most of a typical week?
2. What surprised you when you started?
3. What would you try first if you wanted to learn more about this work?

After the call, write down **one observation**, **one assumption still to check**, and **one follow-up action**. Treat a single person's account as one example of how a role can work.
