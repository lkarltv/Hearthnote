# Session 1

**Date:** July 11, 2026

## Arrival

Samira is returning from a long period of exhaustion and wants to rebuild ambition without rebuilding the conditions that caused burnout.

## What we explored

We distinguished capacity from motivation. Samira’s motivation is returning faster than her capacity, which makes every good idea feel like an obligation.

Three signs of sustainable momentum emerged:

- Work ends at a recognizable time
- Curiosity remains after a demanding day
- Rest is planned before it becomes urgent

## Commitments

- [ ] Choose one meaningful project for the next month
- [ ] Set an end-of-day ritual at 17:30
- [ ] Leave one evening completely unplanned
