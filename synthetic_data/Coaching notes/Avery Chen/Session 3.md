# Session 3

**Date:** October 23, 2026

## Arrival

Avery described the past three weeks as calmer and slightly unfamiliar. Delegating the reporting pack went well, although they checked the work more often than was useful.

## What we explored

The focus shifted from delegation as task transfer to delegation as trust-building. We drew a small ladder:

1. Describe the desired outcome
2. Agree on constraints and check-in points
3. Let the other person choose the route
4. Review for learning, not proof

There was a meaningful pause around step three. Avery recognized that control has been doing the job of reassurance.

## Commitments

- [ ] Agree on one check-in point before handing over work
- [ ] Keep a short “evidence of trust” log
- [ ] Celebrate the team’s decisions publicly

## Threads for next time

Explore the identity shift from reliable individual contributor to leader who creates reliability around them.
